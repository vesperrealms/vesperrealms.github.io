Annihilation: Sad Dark Epic Music

Watch on YouTube

Annihilation is a sad, dark epic track that opens Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms, the story of someone who loses everything and comes back stronger. Beside a mountain lake a single lantern marks home. A comet crosses the sky, the clouds turn red and fire rains down on the valley until nothing is left. Cold rain, grief, and then a pillar of wrath. At grey dawn the lantern goes out after the last piano note.

Fire falls heavier with every chorus, far shores burn and mirror in the lake, rings race across the water on each braam, and the lantern dies exactly on the final note.

Demon Summoning: Dark Ritual Epic Music

Watch on YouTube

Demon Summoning is a dark ritual trance from Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms: sitar drone, hand drum and one theme that returns round after round on new voices. In a volcano crater a seal writes itself on the ground, ring by ring. The demon of rage answers as a column of fire, the demon of hatred as violet lightning, and at the end all the voices are sung together.

Each round draws another ring of the seal, fire twists out of the circle on the rage, violet curtains and lightning belong to the hatred, and the final three rounds burn all at once.

Track 4 of Crowned by Ruin. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

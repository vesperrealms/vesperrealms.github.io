One Sword: Epic Samurai Duel Music

One Sword is an epic samurai duel in music from Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms: shakuhachi, koto, shamisen, huge drums and long silences. On a windy field one dark monolith waits, the enemy of a former life. The sky lowers with every step, even the wind stops, then the charge, and a single stroke that cuts the heavens open.

Pressure grows by darkness and cloud, wind trails streak past on the charge, the white cut lands on the one beat everything was built for, and a hundred golden fragments fall, each note on another instrument.

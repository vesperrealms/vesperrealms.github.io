Might of the Earth: Dark Epic Organ & Choir Music

Watch on YouTube

Might of the Earth is a dark, ominous epic track from Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms: a black-mass organ, a lullaby music box from the depths, bone-like xylophone and death bells over a crushing choir. At midnight in a black basalt crater, a forgotten knight stands on a ledge as a colossal mass of darkness and fire rises out of a lava fissure, red lightning crawling through the storm above.

The mass swells with every organ chord, lava veins glow through the basalt on each braam, shockwaves burst on the death bells, and cold blue will-o'-the-wisps drift up when the music box sings from the abyss.

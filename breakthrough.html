Breakthrough: Epic Uplifting Music

Watch on YouTube

Breakthrough is a driving, uplifting epic track from Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms, with a guitar hook that bounces in 3+3+2, pounding four-on-the-floor drums, taiko and soaring strings. In a flowering Alpine valley, a herd of thirteen wild horses led by a golden Akhal-Teke stallion trots through grey rain, then bursts into sunlight at full gallop as a double rainbow opens over the meadows, running on into a low golden evening and a starlit dusk.

Every hoofbeat lands with the music: the herd trots on the verses and gallops on every drop, dust kicks up on the kick drum, the sky pulses with the beat, and petals and pollen drift on the guitar hook.

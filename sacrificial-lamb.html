Sacrificial Lamb: Sacred Baroque Epic Music

Watch on YouTube

Sacrificial Lamb is a sacred baroque epic from Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms: recorder, oboe, harpsichord and a walking bass, then a storm of string sixteenths. The scales of the world have lost their balance, so heaven sends a lamb of white-gold light down to a holy meadow. Darkness closes over it, devours it, and the hymn rises again in other hands.

Two rings of light tip like scales in an exact musical mirror, the lamb descends with the recorder, lightning strikes on the sentence, and the light is drawn up in a golden spiral during the mass.

Track 5 of Crowned by Ruin. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

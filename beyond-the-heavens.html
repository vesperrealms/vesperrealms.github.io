Beyond the Heavens: Epic Celestial Choir Music

Watch on YouTube

Beyond the Heavens is a luminous, awe-filled epic cinematic track from Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms. At dawn, on a snowy rock above an endless sea of clouds, a mother holds her child and faces the rising sun, as four golden halos turn slowly over the clouds and a pillar of sacred light descends from the sky.

The light swells with every choir and organ chord, the halos pulse on each bell and braam, and at the end everything dissolves into celesta and silence.

Track 3 of Realms of Majesty. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

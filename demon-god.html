Demon God: Epic Symphonic Metal

Watch on YouTube

Demon God is a passacaglia that becomes symphonic metal, from Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms. One bass line of eight bars never changes while one voice after another enters with its own melody. On burnt ground every soul, step and swarm is drawn into a single throne of stone, the realm of demons becomes one person, and even heaven trembles.

Glowing cracks breathe with the bass, light runs to the throne on each step, embers swarm, the dark mass grows with every metal variation, and everything ends as one column of fire.

Track 9 of Crowned by Ruin. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

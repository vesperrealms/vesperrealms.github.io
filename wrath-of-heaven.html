Wrath of Heaven is a powerful epic battle track from Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms, built on taiko drums, brass braams and a soaring orchestra. On a moonlit stone terrace above a sea of mist and karst pillars, a terracotta general stands before 143 soldiers as a giant celestial seal writes itself across the sky, and a golden pillar of heaven's might strikes the altar.

Every taiko hit sends a golden ripple across the stones, glowing kintsugi veins wake the army rank by rank, and sky lanterns rise between the pillars as the music reaches its throne.

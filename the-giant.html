The Giant: Epic Boss Music

Watch on YouTube

The Giant is a heavy, majestic boss theme from Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms: half-time weight, low horns and tuba, every step felt before it is heard. On a misty plateau of mossy stones a girl waits as a thirty-metre elephant walks out of the fog, spiralling closer. Ancient veins of cyan light wake under its skin, it rears over her, then bows its head to a small celesta melody, and walks back into the mist.

Every footstep lands on a beat, dust rises on 160 measured footfalls, the veins flare on the hook and the braams, and the light beats softly with the celesta when the giant stands before her.

Track 8 of Realms of Majesty. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

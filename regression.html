Regression: Epic Second Chance Music

Watch on YouTube

Regression is a galloping folk-epic track from Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms. He rides alone into the storm, ready to die, and one stroke of a blade ends it. Darkness. Then a colossal clock of runes turns backwards across the sky, the rain falls upward, and he wakes at dawn in the past. This time he rides with a vow.

The camera is the rider and the triplet gallop is the horse: lightning lands on the peak, the blade cuts the picture on the orchestra hit, and while time runs back the melody is played from its last note to its first.

Track 2 of Crowned by Ruin. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

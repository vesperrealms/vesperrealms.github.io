Limitless is a soaring, hopeful epic anthem from Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms, with drawbar organ, strummed steel guitar, horns, strings and a hook that keeps climbing. The light grows with the song, the sun climbs with every chorus, and golden dust trails the wings on each drum hit.

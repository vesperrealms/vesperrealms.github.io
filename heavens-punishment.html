Heaven's Punishment: Dark Epic Waltz & Music Box

Watch on YouTube

Heaven's Punishment is a dark epic waltz from Crowned by Ruin by Vesper Realms. In a garden at dusk three lanterns are a family, dancing to a music box, piano, accordion and fiddle. Then a golden seal opens in the sky. Three bells, three lanterns go dark. What returns is the same waltz in minor: lightning, a red seal and fire raining on the garden, until only a broken music box remains.

Fireflies turn in three-four time, each bell puts out one lantern, the seal burns red on the dance of death, and half of the last music box is silence.

Track 8 of Crowned by Ruin. No lyrics, good for gaming, D&D, writing and focus.

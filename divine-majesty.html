Divine Majesty: Epic Rebirth Music

Watch on YouTube

Divine Majesty is the epic finale of Realms of Majesty by Vesper Realms: a story of adversity, death and rebirth told on a single tonic. On a battlefield of ash, under a rain of fire, a lone samurai stands with embers beating in his chest. His heart slows, he turns to ash and the whole world loses its colour. Then one heartbeat returns, wings of phoenix fire open behind him, and a pillar of divine light splits the storm.

The embers beat on the heartbeat drum, the fire rain swells with the choir, four bars of near silence leave a single spark, and every braam of the finale pushes a ring of light across the ground.
